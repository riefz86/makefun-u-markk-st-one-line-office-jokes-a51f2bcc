## The joke

> The office birthday card has been circulating for signatures since Tuesday, and Legal just flagged it as a hostage situation.

The birthday card is a real detail of office life everyone recognises at once — the card that travels desk to desk, waiting for signatures, for days. The laugh lands in the last few words, where Legal, the department that exists to worry about liability, flags the card as a hostage situation: a cheerful ritual of goodwill re-described as a crime scene.

## How it works

`main.py` keeps a small catalogue of candidate one-liners, scores each one against the judge's published criteria (single-line joke about office life, real office detail, laugh in the last few words, clear on one reading, fresh wording), picks the best, validates it against the gate (1–199 characters, exactly one line), and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
